RCLC Sound Studio

Build a truck sound from 5 engine holds and 5 exhaust holds. Drop each recording (a WAV or the video itself) into its box. The studio measures the real rpm, finds the steadiest part, cuts a clean loop, plays it the way the RCLC D15 board does, then saves the finished pack straight onto your SD card. Your files never leave this computer.

1The engine

The type matters: a two-stroke fires every cylinder every turn, so its rpm is measured differently.

2The 5×5 holds

Each box wants one recording of the engine held steady at that rpm, about 15 seconds. Drop the file on the box or press it to choose one. Videos work.

3Extras

Drop start, shutdown, jake, horn and air-brake recordings here

4Save to the SD card

  1. Put the truck's SD card in this computer.
  2. Press Save to SD card and pick the card (the top of it, not a folder inside).
  3. Put the card back in the truck and power up. It plays your new pack.

A pack already on the card is kept as a backup (packs/rclc_backup_...), so you can always go back. Works in Chrome or Edge.
